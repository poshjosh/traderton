User Agreement

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Traderton ("we", "our", or "us") provides trading infrastructure for AI/LLM agents: venue integrations, order execution, risk enforcement, and reconciliation, exposed to connected operator platforms over a secure, machine-to-machine boundary. This agreement covers only what Traderton itself does — executing instructions it receives from a connected operator platform against trading venues. It does not cover the account, authentication, or agent-instruction handling of any operator platform that integrates with Traderton; that is governed by the operator platform's own user agreement. If you are an end user or operator interacting with Traderton only through a connected platform, this agreement applies to Traderton's execution of your trading instructions.

Trading decisions

You are solely responsible for all trading decisions executed through Traderton, including the strategies, instructions, and configuration that produce them. Traderton executes instructions in accordance with the configuration and execution mode selected by the connected operator platform or agent. Traderton does not advise, recommend, or validate trading decisions or strategies.

Trading risk

Trading carries a significant risk of financial loss. Do not trade with funds you cannot afford to lose. Past performance of an agent, bot, or strategy does not guarantee future results.

No investment advice

Traderton is a trading-infrastructure provider, not a financial advisor. Nothing provided by Traderton — including order execution, risk gate decisions, reconciliation data, or any other output — constitutes investment advice.

No guarantees

We do not guarantee the availability, reliability, accuracy, completeness, or performance of Traderton, market data, venue connectivity, or any trading venue we integrate.

Execution modes

Traderton supports the following execution modes:

  • Test — Simulated trading. No real orders are placed.
  • Live — Real orders are placed on supported trading venues (Hyperliquid, Bybit, Jupiter, and 1inch) using real funds.

You are responsible for verifying which execution mode an agent or bot is operating in before trading begins.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Traderton shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use of Traderton, including but not limited to trading losses or data loss.
