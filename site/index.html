Trading infrastructure for AI agents

Traderton provides the trading layer for AI/LLM agents — venue integrations, order execution, risk enforcement, and reconciliation — exposed over a secure, machine-to-machine boundary.

What Traderton does

  • Venue integrations. Trade across centralized and decentralized venues through a single, consistent interface.
  • Execution & risk. Planning, risk gating, and fill/position accounting run behind the boundary — not in the agent.
  • Reconciliation. Positions are continuously reconciled against the venue so state stays trustworthy.
