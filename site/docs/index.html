Trading venues

Traderton connects agents to four venues across the crypto ecosystem. Each guide covers what the venue is good for, how it works, fees, and limitations.

  • Trading venues overview — compare Hyperliquid, Bybit, Jupiter, and 1inch and pick one.
  • Hyperliquid — decentralized perpetual futures; leverage and shorting.
  • Bybit — centralized exchange; spot and futures, fiat on-ramp.
  • Jupiter — DEX aggregator on Solana; fast, cheap token swaps.
  • 1inch — DEX aggregator across EVM chains; best multi-chain routes.
  • Funding your wallets — how to fund platform-generated and user-provided wallets per venue.
